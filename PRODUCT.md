# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

US health, fitness and wellness businesses buying marketing services online: gyms and studios, online coaches and personal trainers, wellness product brands, and fitness/wellness influencers. They arrive wanting more members, clients, sales or followers and decide whether to buy a fixed-price package on the spot. A secondary reader is the payment provider's underwriting reviewer (Razorpay), who checks for clear pricing, working links, business identity and legal policies.

## Product Purpose

Storefront for Digi Solution LLC, a digital marketing and brand growth company for the health, fitness and wellness industry. It sells fixed-price marketing packages with online checkout (Razorpay) and publishes the legal policies required for payment-account activation. Success: a visitor understands what they get and what it costs, and buys or makes contact; the payment provider approves the account.

## Positioning

Specialist, not generalist: marketing only for health, fitness and wellness, sold as transparent fixed-price packages bought online instead of hidden "book a call" quotes.

## Operating Context

US audience, prices in USD. Company: DIGI SOLUTION, LLC, New Mexico LLC (Entity ID 0008045735, formed August 26, 2025), 8206 Louisiana Blvd NE, Ste A #4111, Albuquerque, NM 87113. Domain digisolutionllc.online; email on Hostinger. Payment-provider review asked for clearly displayed pricing and working social links.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Next.js 16 App Router site. Six packages in `lib/site.js` (user confirmed keeping them): Ad Campaign Setup $249, Ad Creative Pack $199, Social Media Starter $399/mo, Paid Ads Management $699/mo, Influencer Promotion Campaign $599, Brand Growth Pro $1,299/mo. Checkout: `/api/order` creates a Razorpay order from the server-side price, `/api/verify` checks the signature. Monthly packages do not auto-renew. Required pages: Pricing, About, Contact, Terms, Privacy, Refund & Cancellation, Shipping & Delivery. Social links empty until real URLs exist. Hosting account not chosen yet.

## Brand Commitments

Name "Digi Solution LLC". User asked for a fully premium feel for a US audience, with minimal premium icons and a strong colour and type choice. Standing preference: bright, Apple-style clean (light grounds, real photography); the user rejected a dark, text-led design. Logo files supplied by the user: `DDigi Solution Wellness Logo.png` (lockup, transparent, dark ink; wordmark reads "DDigi", spelling unconfirmed) and `Modern D-Shaped Leaf Wellness Icon.png` (app icon, white and green D on black). Derived assets live in `public/brand/` and `app/icon.png`.

## Evidence on Hand

None. No testimonials, client logos, case studies, result numbers or photography. Do not fabricate any of them; no "trusted by" rows, ratings or invented stats.

## Product Principles

1. Price and scope are visible before any commitment.
2. Specialist credibility comes from specificity about the wellness industry, not from claims.
3. Every legal and business-identity detail a reviewer looks for is one click away.
4. No promised results; honest about ad spend and third-party costs.
